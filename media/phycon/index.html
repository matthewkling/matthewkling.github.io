Phylogeny & conservation

The California Plant Phylodiversity Atlas

Open the app

To accompany a recent paper on conservation priorities for the California flora, I built the California Plant Phylodiversity Atlas, a web application that lets users interact with the phylogeny, geography, and local community dimensions of our biodiversity dataset. It was a fun Shiny app to put together, including use of the plotly, leaflet, and DT libraries, as well as integration with images from the Jepson eFlora. Check it out and let me know what you think!
